## Tasting Notes

### Appearance
straw
### Aroma
#pear and some #lemon_peel

### Taste
#pear yields quickly to a toasty nutty flavor

### Aftertaste
light pear taste that finishes quickly but coats the tongue

### Overall Impression
I like this. I don’t get as much of the waxy texture as people say. There is some nice complexity as I sip it more. The blend of fruits and nuts are quite nice. It’s milder than many whites but that leaves room to enjoy the flavors.
